01

Who this policy covers

Tommy is Nicholas Chen’s executive assistant AI bot. It reads authorised work information from Discord only and helps summarise it. Nicholas Chen (“we”, “us”, or “the operator”) is responsible for Tommy’s handling of personal information and is the privacy contact for this notice.

This policy covers information processed for Tommy about authorised users, people whose information appears in Discord content, people who contact us, and visitors to these legal pages. You do not need to install or interact with Tommy to have privacy rights concerning information about you.

An organisation that controls a Discord workspace may have its own obligations and privacy notice. Discord and AI providers also process information under their applicable policies. This notice covers our handling of information; it does not replace theirs or treat a server administrator’s permission as consent from every member.

02

Information processed

Tommy processes the following categories only as needed for enabled features and the work requested. Discord is its only work-data source; it does not independently connect to email, calendars, GitHub, or other work applications.

Discord identifiers & context
User IDs, display names, server, channel and message IDs, timestamps, and permission context needed to recognise a request, retrieve authorised material, and deliver a response.
Work & message content
Prompts, messages, relevant conversation history, and any supported attachments explicitly provided through Discord for the requested work. Content can include the names, opinions, tasks, and contact details of other people.
Generated information
Summaries, answers, suggested follow-up items, source references, and feedback on an output. An AI-generated statement about someone can itself be personal information and can be incorrect.
Access settings
Authorised Discord source and destination settings and application credentials needed to operate the bot. Tommy does not request your Discord password or user-account token.
Operational & support records
Limited request metadata, errors, security events, and information you include in a support or privacy request. These records support operation, troubleshooting, and request handling; they are not a separate archive of source messages.
Website requests
A hosting provider may receive an IP address, requested URL, browser information, and request time when serving these pages. The pages themselves have no tracking scripts or forms.

Sources are your requests, Discord’s authorised interfaces, other participants in the permitted Discord material, and the results of processing it. A link in a message does not by itself authorise Tommy to sign in to or retrieve records from an external work service. Tommy does not obtain information from data brokers.

Do not provide secrets, protected health information, financial information, or other sensitive information prohibited by Discord’s rules or your organisation’s policies. If such information is inadvertently included, contact us so access and deletion can be addressed.

03

Purposes & legal grounds

We use information to retrieve authorised Discord material, generate and deliver the requested assistance, maintain permitted sources and destinations, troubleshoot failures, secure access, respond to requests, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not repurpose it for unrelated monitoring.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we rely on performance of our agreement with an authorised user for the requested assistance; necessary and proportionate legitimate interests in operating and securing the Service where allowed and balanced against people’s rights; consent where required; and legal obligations for legally required processing. A consent-based activity can be stopped by withdrawing consent.

Processing information about other participants is not automatically covered by our agreement with the requesting user. Appropriate notice and a lawful basis, such as valid consent or a legally permitted, balanced legitimate interest in summarising authorised work discussions, are needed separately. We do not infer consent from technical access, and must exclude information where the necessary basis or permission is absent.

We do not sell personal information, share it for targeted advertising, build personal profiles from Discord data, or make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Tommy provides assistance for human review.

04

Access to Discord

Tommy processes requests and relevant content from Discord conversations it is authorised to access. Depending on the requested work and granted permissions, that can include conversation history rather than only messages that mention the bot. It does not have a general right to read every server or private conversation.

We limit reading to the approved scope needed for the requested work. Technical channel permissions can expose messages from people who have never invoked Tommy. Server administrators must make the bot’s presence and reading scope clear and support appropriate restrictions and requests from affected people.

Authorising a summary does not authorise Tommy to modify source content, sign in to other work applications, or perform unrelated actions.

The destination matters. A private source can become visible to more people if its summary is posted to a shared channel. Tommy must deliver information only to authorised recipients, and users must choose an appropriate destination. A Discord message is also subject to Discord’s handling and the access rules of its destination.

Removing access prevents subsequent reading through those permissions. It does not recall a message already delivered to Discord or a copy another recipient has made. See your controls for deletion requests.

05

How AI processing works

Tommy uses OpenAI’s Codex, Anthropic’s Claude, and local models as its AI options. To create a summary or answer, the selected system may process your instruction together with relevant excerpts, source metadata, and conversation context. When a hosted service is selected, that context is sent to its provider. The output is returned to the authorised destination in Discord.

OpenAI / Codex
When Codex uses OpenAI-hosted inference, OpenAI receives the submitted context. The applicable product, account, and settings determine its handling. OpenAI’s API data controls describe API retention and training settings; they should not be assumed to describe every Codex subscription or feature.
Anthropic / Claude
When Claude uses Anthropic-hosted inference, Anthropic receives the submitted context. Commercial and consumer products have different training arrangements, and retention depends on the service and settings used.
Local models
Local inference runs on the operator’s configured hardware. The model publisher does not receive a prompt merely because its downloaded model is used. Local execution does not eliminate local logs or storage, and any external telemetry, tool, or hosted fallback that receives content needs separate disclosure and authorisation.

The selected route depends on the request and the operator’s configuration. Listing an option here does not mean every request is sent to every provider. Context is shared only with the route or routes needed for the authorised work. A request designated for local-only processing must not silently fall back to hosted inference; a change requires authorisation.

We use models for inference—generating a response to a request. We do not train or fine-tune models with Discord API message content or opt that content into provider model-improvement programmes. Provider selection, account settings, and feedback workflows must support this commitment.

Hosted providers can retain or review information under the rules for the product, account, and features used, including for safety or legal obligations. A “no training” setting is not a promise of zero storage or no human access. Running a Codex or Claude client on local hardware does not make its hosted inference local. Contact us for the route and settings relevant to your information.

AI can generate incorrect personal information or unsupported inferences. Request correction or deletion if an output misrepresents you. We do not treat a generated summary as an authoritative record about a person.

06

Who receives information

Information may be made available only as needed to:

  • Authorised recipients: the user or permitted audience receiving a requested result. An instruction to deliver data does not override another person’s access restrictions.
  • Necessary service providers: the selected AI provider and any disclosed infrastructure provider acting for Tommy under appropriate restrictions. Adding a recipient requires an updated notice and any necessary permission before content is sent.
  • The operator and authorised support personnel: to resolve a specific request, investigate a security problem, or maintain the Service, with access limited to what is needed.
  • Legally required recipients: when disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal process, limited to the information required.

Discord receives messages and interactions delivered through its platform and handles them under its Privacy Policy. Its original messages and other records exist independently of Tommy’s processing copies.

We do not sell, license, or share Discord API data with advertising networks, data brokers, or monetisation services. No general permission to transfer that data in a business sale is created by this notice; any transfer must separately satisfy applicable law and Discord’s rules.

08

Retention & deletion

Tommy keeps source content and summaries only while needed for the requested work, then deletes them. The retention period is tied to that work, rather than a fixed number of days or indefinite assistant memory. When the work is completed, abandoned, cancelled, or no longer needs particular content, that content and its working copies are deleted promptly.

This rule applies to data under the operator’s control. A valid deletion request or closure of the Service also triggers deletion, unless a specific legal obligation requires a limited record to be retained. Removing permission and deleting existing data are separate operations.

Source content & prompts
Held only for the requested task, including follow-up work you explicitly request. Processing copies, temporary files, and caches are deleted when no longer needed for that work.
Summaries & working context
Bot-held summaries and any derived working context follow the same deletion rule. They are not retained as an indefinite archive or ongoing assistant memory. Copies delivered to Discord are addressed separately below.
Access settings & credentials
Kept while needed to maintain an authorised installation or access permission. Removed or revoked when the relevant access ends or they are no longer needed.
Logs & support records
Limited metadata is retained only while necessary to operate the Service, resolve a specific issue, handle a request, or meet a legal obligation. Source content and summaries are not kept in logs to avoid the task-based deletion rule.
Local copies & backups
Deletion must cover operator-controlled working copies and derived data, not just a primary record. Work content must not be kept in an unrelated backup or restored after deletion merely because it was previously processed.
Hosted AI providers
OpenAI or Anthropic may retain submitted data according to the product, account settings, and applicable requirements. Deletion of Tommy’s local copy does not instantly delete a provider’s copy. We use available deletion controls and communicate any limits relevant to your request.
Messages in Discord
Original messages, delivered summaries, and recipient copies can remain in Discord under its own rules. Tommy’s task-based deletion rule concerns bot-held processing copies; it is not a promise to erase a Discord conversation. We address output messages under our control where possible when deletion is requested.

If a legal obligation requires keeping a particular record, it should be isolated, access restricted, and use limited to that obligation until deletion is allowed. We will explain relevant limits on a deletion request unless legally prohibited.

09

Your controls & rights

Stop future access

Remove Tommy from a server you administer, restrict its channel access, or revoke its Discord application authorisation where available. If you do not control those settings, ask the administrator or contact us to stop processing your information. Simply no longer sending requests does not remove existing channel permissions. Revoking access can make related features unavailable.

Ask about existing information

Email m@nixholas.me with the subject “Tommy privacy request”. State whether you want access, correction, deletion, or help limiting processing. Include your Discord user ID or other relevant account identifier and, if useful, a message link or approximate date. Do not include passwords, access tokens, or a full copy of sensitive work material.

We may ask for proportionate information to verify that a request concerns you or that you are authorised to act for someone else. We will use that information to handle the request, avoid exposing other people’s data, and respond within the period required by applicable law. If we cannot comply fully, we will explain the reason and available next steps. This route is also available to people who do not use Tommy.

Rights that may apply

Depending on your location and the law applicable to the processing, you may have rights to know about and access your information; correct inaccuracies; obtain a portable copy; request deletion; object to or restrict processing; withdraw consent; and complain to a relevant privacy regulator. Withdrawing consent does not make earlier lawful processing unlawful. We will not penalise you for exercising a protected right, though some features cannot work without necessary data.

For example, where applicable, Singapore’s PDPA provides access, correction, and consent-related protections, and EEA or UK laws provide additional rights described above. A relevant regulator may be Singapore’s Personal Data Protection Commission or your local data-protection authority. This notice does not restrict any rights available under another applicable law.

10

Security

Our security commitments are to limit permissions and operator access, protect credentials, encrypt data in transit and retained Discord API data at rest, restrict provider access to its necessary purpose, and apply the deletion rules above. Safeguards must be appropriate to the information and processing involved; this policy does not claim a particular certification or independent audit.

Security also depends on Discord settings and the permissions of output destinations. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. A concern should be reported promptly to m@nixholas.me without including secrets.

If information is accessed or disclosed without authorisation, we will investigate, take appropriate containment and remediation steps, and notify affected people, Discord, and authorities as required by the applicable rules and law.

11

International processing

Discord, OpenAI, and Anthropic may process information outside your country, including in countries where they or their service providers operate. Hosted processing locations depend on the selected product and account configuration. Local inference takes place on the operator’s configured hardware; use of a local model does not change Discord’s separate processing of messages.

Where applicable law requires safeguards for an international transfer, we must establish suitable protections before transferring the information, such as an applicable adequacy decision or approved contractual safeguards with any necessary additional measures. Contact us for information about the locations and safeguards applicable to your request. We do not promise that every request stays within a particular country.

12

This website & cookies

These standalone legal pages use locally served fonts, styling, and a small theme script. The script reads and saves only your light or dark theme preference in your browser’s local storage, shared with the blog on the same website. The pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, third-party embeds, or cookies set by page code. Reading them does not connect your Discord account to Tommy.

The hosting platform may process ordinary web request data to deliver and secure the pages. Its logs and retention are separate from the bot’s processing. Clicking an external link takes you to that site’s practices. Opening an email link uses your email application, and sending a message shares the information you include with the operator and the email services involved.

13

Children’s information

Tommy is intended for authorised adults doing work and is not directed to children. Users must be at least 18 under the Terms of Service. The Service must not knowingly obtain Discord API data about people below Discord’s minimum age or the higher minimum required in their country.

Do not provide children’s personal information for Tommy to process. If you believe such information has been included, contact us so we can investigate, stop inappropriate processing, and remove information that should not have been collected.

14

Policy updates

Revisions to this policy will show a new update date. Material changes to purposes, recipients, retention, or access scope require appropriate advance notice through the Service or another authorised channel, and fresh consent where required. Connecting an additional work service would require revising this Discord-only scope first.

Publishing a revised notice or continuing to use the bot does not by itself authorise incompatible new processing of previously collected information. If a proposed use requires additional permission, we must obtain it before that use starts.
